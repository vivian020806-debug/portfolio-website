Frequently Asked Questions

What types of projects do you work on?

I specialize in brand visual design, including logo design, brand identity (VI), poster design, and visual extensions. I also help with e-commerce visual design and have experience in content strategy and AI-assisted design projects.

What is your typical turnaround time?

Turnaround time depends on the project scope. For smaller projects, I typically deliver within 1-2 weeks. Larger projects may take 3-4 weeks or more. We can discuss timelines during our initial consultation.

Do you use AI in your design process?

Yes, I integrate AI tools into my design workflow to enhance creativity and speed up iteration. However, AI is a tool to augment my design thinking, not replace it. The final output always reflects professional design judgment and quality standards.

What is your pricing structure?

My pricing varies based on project scope, complexity, and timeline. I offer flexible options including hourly rates, project-based pricing, and retainer arrangements. Let's discuss your specific needs to find the right pricing model.

Can you work with international clients?

Absolutely! I have experience working with international clients and across different time zones. I'm comfortable communicating in both English and Chinese.
